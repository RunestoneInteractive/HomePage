Exciting news! Runestone Academy received funding from the GenAI in CS Consortium to support educators in strategically integrating genAI into their teaching methods and curricula. The consortium is a destination for academics seeking example courses and overall guidance. **Check it out at** [TeachCSwithAI.org](http://TeachCSwithAI.org)

Our first project will be to integrate Runestone books with [Learning Clues](https://www.learningclues.com/) to provide secure 24x7 tutoring for your students. Students will be able to ask questions, and have a conversation with AI with all of the responses grounded in the content provided on Runestone Academy.
